LEVELS: match every new track to soft_piano.mp3 (about -9.8 LUFS)
================================================================
video_renderer.py applies ONE fixed volume to whichever track it picks. It does
not normalise. So a track mastered quieter does not sound "softer and tasteful",
it sounds broken -- and a louder one drowns the video. Among the candidates
measured for this batch, one sat at -28.8 LUFS, 19 dB below the reference; it
would have been effectively silent behind the footage.

Before adding a track: normalise it to roughly -9.8 LUFS, confirm with
  ffmpeg -i <file> -af ebur128=framelog=quiet -f null -
and only then list it here. A track nobody can account for, or one nobody has
levelled, does not belong in this directory.
